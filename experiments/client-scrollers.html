02 Uppercase — one voice, display caps

All caps in the heavy Kumbh cut on the shared display-caps tracking token — the same voice as the hero lockup and the WORK heading. No serif alternation: one uniform band, so the rhythm comes from the names themselves. Caps have no descenders, so the leading tightens and the three rows sit closer together.
