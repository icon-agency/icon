01 Rotating text lattice

One text strip (~7:1) repeated as an L-shaped column: a wall facing the viewer plus strips folded 90° at its base receding into depth. Columns repeat on a fixed pitch, alternate ones turned −90° in-plane so their ribbons run vertically — a woven lattice of a single sentence. A very long lens flattens the projection toward isometric, and the scene turns rotateY(+θ) rotateX(−θ) for a coupled tumble; wheel over the stage nudges θ. Brand-blue world, white display caps.

02 Kinetic text box

Four planes hinged on the stage’s edges and folded 90° into the screen — the inside of a box seen from its open end, under a short lens for a real perspective plunge. All four faces share the same seven depth bands, so the hairlines ring the box continuously; band height and type size follow one depth ramp, keeping the rows tight at every depth while the type converges with the geometry. Floor and ceiling rows run across and slide sideways, wall rows are rotated 90° and slide along the walls, with directions and speeds alternating per row. A settled lockup sits on the far face at the vanishing point.
