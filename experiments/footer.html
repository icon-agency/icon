01 Type lockup · ARCHIVED

The previous direction, frozen — the footer as it stood before the logo replaced the words, kept so the change has something to be measured against. Styles, markup and behaviour are inlined copies (prefixed exp-footer01) rather than the live component, so this stays a true record after production moves on. Everything is here: the centred LET’S TALK CTA, the newsletter column, the four-office <details> accordion (CSS-only, no JS), the socials and Acknowledgement of Country, and the full-width “Make what matters” lockup. Motion too — the panel grows from the page gutter to full bleed on a scroll timeline, the blocks fade up in reading order and the lockup’s words rise out of their mask on a stagger, and scroll velocity shears the whole thing (the lockup 1.5× harder than the text above it). Scroll into it to see the reveal; scroll fast to see the shear.

02 Logo lockup, no film · ALTERNATE

The same footer as 01 — CTA, newsletter, office accordion, socials, acknowledgement, and the same grow, reveal and scroll-shear — with the ICON logo closing the page instead of “Make what matters” (experiments/footer2.png). The mark is painted as a masked box rather than an <img>: the artwork is dark-on-light, this panel is near-black, so its shape is taken as an alpha mask and the colour comes from --footer-ink underneath. Full width at any viewport comes free here — the box carries the SVG’s own 213:69 aspect ratio, so it is exact geometry rather than a measured constant. The logo rises out of its mask on the same clock the three words used.

03 Films inside the logo · SHIPPING TODAY

Yes — and this is it running. Identical to 02 in every respect; the only change is what sits behind the mask. Instead of a flat --footer-ink fill, the hero’s banner films stack inside the box and cross-fade, clipped to the letterforms. Because 02 already paints the logo as a mask over a fill layer, the whole difference is a modifier class — which is why 03 is not a third copy of the footer. Watch the counters of the C and O: they are the only openings big enough to read footage in, and they are what decides whether this reads as a window or as noise. One real catch, and it is not a technical one — raw footage reads well on the bright slides and vanishes on the dark ones, because dark film inside the letterforms leaves nothing to separate the wordmark from a near-black panel. So this carries a small legibility floor: a brightness lift, plus the slide held just under full opacity so the white fill behind it sets a minimum no frame can go under. Both are one line each in the styles above if you would rather see it unguarded. Videos idle while the panel is off screen, and reduced motion holds a first frame.
