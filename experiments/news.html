01 Pinned horizontal scroller · ARCHIVED

The previous direction, frozen. Styles are an inlined copy (prefixed exp-news01) so this stays a true record even though production has moved on. A tall .news__pin hosts a block view-timeline; while its sticky child covers the viewport the card track translates sideways in lockstep with vertical scroll, then the pin releases. Pure CSS — no ScrollTrigger. Each image carries a split-axis parallax (horizontal drift tracks the pinned sweep, vertical drift tracks the section’s travel). Below ~780px tall in landscape — or under reduced motion, or without scroll-timeline support — the pin is dropped entirely for a native swipe row, because the height-guarded cards would otherwise shrink to thumbnails and the sweep distance can invert. Sideways gestures never chain into page scroll (js/home-c.js).

02 Editorial stack · SHIPPING TODAY

Three rows in normal flow: a square media tile beside a text column whose title sits at the top and whose category/date sits on the tile’s baseline, with the NEWS masthead and the More link pinned to the top and bottom of a left rail. No pin, no scroll-timeline sweep and no swipe fallback — ordinary vertical scrolling, so all of that machinery was deleted rather than left dormant. The images keep a single-axis parallax on a view() timeline, and the cursor-tilt hover carries over from the work tiles.

04 Centred stack · TRY

The rail|list split is what leaves the tall empty column beside the stories. Here the masthead sits centred above them and the list runs as one centred column, so the section reads as a single stack. The list is held to min(100%, 68rem) rather than full bleed — the card is a tile beside a text block, and across the whole page the headline measure gets far too long.

05 Bigger type — work-title size · TRY

Card titles pointed at --voice-mid, the shared mid voice .work__title wears — clamp(1.4rem, 2.1vw, 3.4rem) against the card’s shipping clamp(1.25rem, 1.5vw, 2.5rem). Layout is untouched — and that is the problem. Measured at 1469px: the Butterfly headline TRUNCATES here (content 321px into a 201px box), because .news-card__title is -webkit-line-clamp: 5 to keep the meta on the tile’s baseline, and bigger type reaches five lines sooner in the rail’s 323px measure. The shipping size in the same column fits exactly (143px into 143px), so this direction only works if the clamp is raised or the measure grows — which is what 06 does.

06 Centred + bigger type · TRY

04 and 05 together, since they are the obvious pair: the centred stack recovers the width the rail was spending on nothing, and the bigger title is what that recovered width buys. This is the one that works. Same measurement as 05: the title measure goes 323px → 552px, and the Butterfly headline now fits in four lines with no truncation (160px into 160px). So bigger type is not the trade-off it looks like in 05 — it is affordable, but only once the empty rail is gone.
